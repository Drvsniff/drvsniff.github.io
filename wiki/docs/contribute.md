# Add a page

1. Open the [page maker](maker/index.html) on your phone or laptop.
2. Pick a section and template, write the page, and tick the box.
3. Tap **Create on GitHub**, then **Commit changes**. The wiki rebuilds in about a minute.

To fix a page, tap the pencil icon at the top of it. Without write access, GitHub offers to open a pull request for you.

## House rules

- Say what you flashed: board, firmware name and version.
- Credit the authors and link the source repo.
- Redact SSIDs, MACs and locations that identify a home.
- Methods and your own footprint only. No doxxing.
- Only gear you own or have written permission to test.
