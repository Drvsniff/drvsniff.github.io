What gets posted

ESP32 builds

Marauder, Ghost ESP, Bruce, Cheap Yellow Display, T-Embed, ESP32-S3

Which firmware runs on which board, how to flash over web serial, and why a NEO-6M GPS module never gets a fix indoors. Post your wiring and your serial log, not just the error message.

Linux

Debian, Arch, Kali, Alpine, aircrack-ng, Kismet, udev, WireGuard

Monitor mode on a USB adapter, Kismet on a Pi Zero 2 W, udev rules so your serial port stops changing names, and dotfiles worth stealing. Beginners get answers here, not links to the manual.

Opsec

MAC randomisation, compartmentalised accounts, threat models

Separating your wardriving handle from your real name, what your phone broadcasts when it's in your pocket, and how to tell whether you need a VPN or just better habits.

OSINT

WiGLE queries, certificate transparency, archive.org, photo geolocation

Method write-ups: how a photo was placed on a map, how a domain was traced to its hosting history. Targets are public records and your own footprint. Nobody gets doxxed here.

House rules

  • Passive onlyBeacons, probes, advertisements. No deauth, no evil twin on networks you don't own.
  • Your hardware or written permissionIf you can't show the scope, don't run it.
  • Redact before you postStrip SSIDs and MACs that identify a home from any map or log you share.
  • No find-this-person threadsOSINT here means methods and your own footprint.
  • Credit the authorsLink the source repo when you share a firmware build.
  • Say what you flashedInclude board, firmware name and version when you ask for help.
